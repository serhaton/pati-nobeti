Tested devices and operating systems before submission: iPhone 16 Simulator on iOS 18 (Xcode simulator), and physical iPhone devices running current iOS 18 builds via Expo Dev Client during feature validation.

App description, target audience, problem solved, and value: Pati Uzat is a community-based app for people who organize care for stray animals. It helps local volunteers and community admins coordinate feeding points, track animal-related expenses (food and veterinary), collect and allocate contribution records, manage member approvals, and monitor debt/credit tracking in a shared community budget view. The target audience is animal volunteers, neighborhood/community organizers, and local rescue-support groups. The app solves coordination and transparency problems in shared street-animal care by keeping records, approvals, and supporting documents in one place. The app is not a financial services app. Expense and contribution entries are informational tracking records only and are not directly connected to any bank, card network, payment gateway, wallet, or other financial institution.

Setup and access instructions for core features, including login credentials or sample files: No special reviewer credentials are required. The reviewer can create a new account from the login screen using email registration, then sign in with email/password. After login, create or join a community from the community selection flow. Main features become available when a community is selected. Community admin-only features include member approval/rejection, feeding point management, expense approval, and contribution allocation. If a newly joined user is pending approval, some admin-controlled actions are intentionally restricted until approved. No sample files are required. Optional uploads are user-provided photos/receipts from camera or gallery.

External services, tools, and platforms used for core functionality: Supabase is used for authentication, database, row-level access control, storage of uploaded files, and edge functions. Email/password authentication is used in the current public build. Expo Notifications is used for push notification token handling and delivery flow integration. Expo Location is used for map and location-based feeding point operations. React Native Maps is used to display and interact with map locations. Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) is used for banner ads.

Regional differences: The app functionality is consistent across regions. There are no region-specific feature gates or content variants. Minor practical differences may occur only due to third-party map/geolocation availability and local network conditions, not due to app business logic.

Regulated industry or protected third-party material: This app is not in a highly regulated industry and does not provide regulated services (for example medical, financial advisory, gambling, or legal services). It does not process payments, move money, or connect to financial institutions. The app does not bundle protected third-party media content. User-uploaded content (such as photos/receipts) is provided by users themselves as part of normal app usage.

Third-party analytics: No dedicated third-party analytics SDK is integrated for product analytics in the current build.

Third-party advertising and kids app policy link: Yes, the app includes third-party advertising via Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) banner ads. Public policy references for kids/family-related ad practices are: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6223431 and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

Data sharing with third parties, purposes, and storage: Yes. Data is shared with Supabase to provide core backend operations (authentication, database records, access control, storage, push-event related processing), and with Google AdMob for ad serving. App backend data is stored in Supabase-hosted infrastructure configured by the app owner. Ad-related processing occurs within the AdMob ecosystem according to Google policies.

User/device data collection beyond third-party analytics or advertising: Yes. The app collects and processes data required for core functionality, not for separate analytics profiling. This includes account data (email, auth provider, profile fields such as name/phone when provided), community and membership records, feeding point coordinates and logs, expense/contribution records, uploaded receipts/photos, and notification device tokens for push delivery. Location is used for map-based feeding point interactions and is permission-based. This data is used strictly to provide app features such as authentication, authorization, community workflows, record keeping, file access, and notification delivery.
